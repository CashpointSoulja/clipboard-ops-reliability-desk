# Personas and jobs to be done

> **Hypothesis.** These personas are synthetic and were not built from interviews. Their names are roles, not people.

## P1 · Support Ops Operator (primary user)
- **Context:** triages the support queue during a spike and has authority to run approved playbooks.
- **JTBD:** "When I see many tickets about the same thing, I want to confirm they share a cause and fix them once, safely, so I can get the queue back to normal without creating new problems."
- **Pains (hypothesis):** repeating the same lookup; not knowing whether a fix worked; fear of touching pay or credentials by mistake.
- **In the demo:** the *Ops Operator* role.

## P2 · Marketplace Reliability On-call (incident owner)
- **JTBD:** "When an integration degrades, I want one incident with linked evidence and an audit trail, so I can confirm the blast radius and close it with proof."
- **In the demo:** owner of INC-001. Receives refused-repair handoffs.

## P3 · Specialist reviewers (escalation targets)
- *Billing Ops Specialist*, *Credentialing Review Lead*, *Trust & Safety Reviewer*.
- **JTBD:** "When a case touches pay, credentials or conduct, I want it to reach me with context and no automated decision already made."

## P4 · Viewer / stakeholder (read-only)
- **JTBD:** "I want to see the state of an incident without being able to change anything."
- **In the demo:** the *Viewer (read-only)* role, which shows the permission boundary.

## Indirect beneficiaries
Workplaces and healthcare professionals who see correct shift status sooner. **No claim is made about the size of this benefit.**
